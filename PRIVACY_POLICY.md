## 2. Information that may be involved

### 2.1. Information stored on your device

The Game may store **save files, progress, preferences, language, graphics settings, and control settings** on the player's device to provide the gameplay experience and remember choices. **Local storage alone does not mean this information is transmitted to the Developer.**

You may delete these files using Game or operating-system features where available. Uninstalling an app does not always delete all user files.

### 2.2. Updates, downloads, and external pages

When the Game or its launcher connects to **GitHub** to check versions, download files, or open release notes, the service provider may receive technical connection data such as **IP address, request date and time, browser/application technical identifiers, and device information**. Collection and retention by that provider are governed by its own policy; this does not necessarily mean that the Developer receives those logs.

When you visit the Game's **itch.io** page, itch.io may likewise process information concerning access, accounts, interactions, and, when applicable, purchases made on the platform.

### 2.3. Discord integration

Where available and enabled, **Discord Rich Presence** may share activity-related fields with the Discord application, such as the **Game name, displayed status, activity details, session time, and presence images**, according to the fields actually implemented. This activity may be visible to others depending on your Discord privacy settings.

Activity visibility should also be managed within Discord's privacy settings. Discord's own rules govern its use and retention of this data.

### 2.4. Voluntary communications

If you contact the Developer by email or another provided channel, we may receive **your contact address, provided name, message, voluntarily attached files, and information needed to investigate a reported issue**. Please avoid sending passwords, government identifiers, or unnecessary sensitive information.
